Fried Argentina Beefloaf

Description

Fried Argentina Beefloaf is a simple and delicious Filipino dish made using canned Argentina Beefloaf. It is sliced into pieces and fried until golden brown and crispy. It is commonly served with hot steamed rice, fried egg, and ketchup.

Ingredients

  • 1 can Argentina Beefloaf
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 egg (optional)
  • Ketchup for dipping (optional)

Steps

  1. Open the can of Argentina Beefloaf.
  2. Remove the beefloaf from the can.
  3. Slice the beefloaf into pieces.
  4. Heat cooking oil in a frying pan.
  5. Place the beefloaf slices into the pan.
  6. Fry each side until golden brown.
  7. Remove from the pan.
  8. Place on a plate with paper towels.
  9. Fry an egg if desired.
  10. Serve with hot steamed rice and ketchup.
